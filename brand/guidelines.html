Logo

One house, one word, drawn in dots

The Homebase logo is a dot-matrix house over a dot-matrix HOMEBASE. Every file in this kit is generated from the same dot maps the website renders, so the logo is identical everywhere it appears. Use the stacked lockup first. Reach for the others only when the space calls for them.

Stacked

Primary

The house centred over the wordmark, as on homebase.love and the Open Graph card. Use it by default.

Horizontal

Secondary

The house beside the wordmark at cap height. For short, wide spaces: partner grids, sponsor walls, event banners, nav bars.

House mark

Icon

Seven rows of dots. For avatars, app icons, favicons, stickers and anywhere the name is already present.

Wordmark

The name alone, for very wide, very short placements such as a lanyard or the edge of a stage backdrop.

Construction

Built on a ten-unit grid

Every dot sits on a square grid with a pitch of 10 units and has a diameter of 8, so neighbouring dots are always separated by a gap one quarter of their size. The rhythm of round dots with even gaps is the brand. Never redraw it by hand.

Grid
10 units from dot centre to dot centre.
Dot
8 units across, 80% of the pitch. The gap is 2 units.
House
7 × 7 cells: a single-dot chimney point, a stepped roof, eaves one dot wider each side, and a door one dot wide and two tall.
Letters
Each letter is a solid block 11 cells wide and 15 tall, traced to its outline, which is why every stroke reads as two parallel lines of dots. Letters sit 3 cells apart.
Stacked
The house is drawn at 3.2 × the wordmark’s scale, centred, with a gap of 7.7 wordmark cells between the two.
Horizontal
The house is as tall as the letters and sits 6 cells from the H.
Clear space and size

Give it room to breathe

Keep a margin of at least one fifth of the logo’s height clear on every side, free of text, edges and other marks. The white-on-blue files already include this margin.

Interface

Round, soft and friendly

The shapes echo the dots. Buttons and chips are full pills, cards have a 16 px radius and a soft shadow, and every page opens with the blue masthead that curves away into white.

The masthead curve

A wide white ellipse cuts into the bottom of the blue, so the page lands softly instead of on a hard edge. Use it on web pages, slide covers and event signage.

One primary blue button per card or dialog. Every other action is quiet.

Voice

Warm, plain and specific

Homebase sounds like a friend who runs a good house: welcoming, direct, and always concrete. Name the city, the date, the number. Use short sentences. Leave out hype.

Misuse

Keep the dots as they are

Always start from a file in this kit. These are the mistakes that come up most often.

Don’t stretch or squash it

Scale it only in proportion.

Don’t recolour it

Blue, white or ink only.

Don’t rotate it

The house always stands upright.

Don’t add effects

No shadows, glows, outlines or gradients.

Don’t lose the contrast

On busy images use white on a blue panel.

Files

What’s in the kit

Every logo comes in four colours and three formats. SVG for the web and design tools, PDF for print shops, PNG for everything else. The PNGs are 4096 or 8192 px on their long side, enough to print about 35 cm wide at 300 dpi. The kit lives in the brand/ folder of the website repository, and bun scripts/brand-kit.ts rebuilds it from the site’s own dot maps.
