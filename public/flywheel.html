How to read it

Volume is where the loop starts: lockers trade $home, every trade pays a creator fee, and 100% of it funds Based House. The base is today: 200 lockers trading about $28 of $home a day each, which is 5 ETH a year. 5 ETH funds a residency of 15; a residency launches one founder and draws one from outside; every claim needs a lock of 10,000 $home for a year, so each launch’s 50 claimers join the pool. Attention is the multiplier: at 5×, a residency and its two launches reach 2,800 people, and one in four of them lock, which takes the pool from 200 to 1,000 in the first year. The number in the small loop is the wheel’s growth for the year on the ring: tap it to raise the attention, each step reaching 800 more people per residency, and the switch under it shows the locks alone.
