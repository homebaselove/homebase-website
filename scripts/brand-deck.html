Construction

Built on a ten-unit grid

{{construction}}
Grid
10 units from dot centre to dot centre.
Dot
8 units across, 80% of the pitch. The gap is 2 units.
House
7 × 7 cells: a one-dot peak, a stepped roof, eaves one dot wider each side, and a door one dot wide and two tall.
Letters
Solid blocks 15 cells tall and 11 wide (13 for the M), traced to their outline, so every stroke reads as two lines of dots. Letters sit 3 cells apart.
Stacked
The house at 3.3 × the wordmark’s scale, centred, 7.7 wordmark cells above it.
Horizontal
The house as tall as the letters, 6 cells from the H.
